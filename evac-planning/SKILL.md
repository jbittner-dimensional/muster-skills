# Evacuation Planning

Evac planning is a **pure composition** of what the graph already holds — it adds
no new ingest. You are counting, not collecting.

## What to compose
- **Households** and **pet households** in scope.
- **Total pets, by species** — the crate-and-kennel math.
- **Unconfirmed households** — the work list a planner has to chase down.

## Rules
1. Everything is read from the living graph; nothing is hand-entered.
2. Reconcile scopes — a station's numbers must roll up cleanly into the enterprise.
3. Gate the panel behind **certification**: a planning view goes live only when an
   officer has certified it onto the board.

Pairs with [pets-and-dependents](../pets-and-dependents) for the household/animal
counts.
