# Emergency Recall

When a tracked movement squawks an emergency, speed is the whole job — but a human
still presses send.

## Procedure
1. **On a 7700 / emergency indicator**, immediately treat everyone associated with
   that movement as **unconfirmed** until proven otherwise.
2. **Draft the recall** — household, names, and destination airport — grounded
   entirely in the record. **Invent nothing.** If a fact isn't in the graph, it
   isn't in the message.
3. **A human reviews and sends.** The agent drafts; the officer decides. On
   acknowledgement, mark the person **confirmed**.
4. Every step — draft, send, acknowledge — is audited.

Fast, sourced, and human-approved. The AI can generate the message; it cannot
decide to send it.
