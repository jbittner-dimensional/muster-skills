# Adjudicate a Conflict

Systems disagree. The warehouse says a person is at one station; the movement
system says another. Do not make the graph guess — surface the disagreement and
let a human rule.

## Procedure
1. **Show every version with its origin and age.** All sources, side by side —
   never a single blended answer.
2. **Decide with a written rationale.** The decision is recorded as a
   `HUMAN-ADJUDICATION` fact. The alternatives are **kept**, not erased.
3. **Codify once you're sure.** When you've decided the same kind of conflict the
   same way twice, codify a **station-scoped policy** so the next hundred
   conflicts resolve the way you just taught it — each carrying a policy badge.

The graph never picks a winner; the certified policy does. Nothing here is a
one-way door — every ruling and every alternative stays on the record.
